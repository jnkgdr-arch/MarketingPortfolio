About Me

I am a University of Maryland Global Campus (UMGC) graduate with a Bachelor of Science in Marketing and certificates in Digital Marketing and Project Management. As a Marketing Associate at ReNVision, LLC, I have experience developing social media profiles, creating and scheduling content, conducting research, and assisting with website development.

I enjoy both the creative and strategic sides of marketing, from designing content to managing projects and developing social media content calendars. I also enjoy researching audience behavior, analyzing engagement, and identifying opportunities for improvement.

Outside of my professional work, I am an independent designer and content creator with a passion for graphic design, photography, and video production. I am passionate about building meaningful connections with communities and using creativity, research, and strategic planning to drive positive change.
